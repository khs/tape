## What's outside the chart grid

A few things this dashboard's tiles can't demonstrate inline, open
the relevant page in another tab to play with them.

### Composer (top right → **Compose**)

The composer is where you build a custom dashboard:

- **Sources tab**: every individual data series we have (~9,000 of
  them: FRED economic series, Yahoo equities + futures, ACS demographics
  by congressional district / state / metro / county / tract / block
  group, World Bank country indicators, BLS subseries, Treasury TIC,
  USAspending federal flows). Click any source to drop it into your
  active section as a single-series tile.
- **Pregenerated charts tab**: curated multi-source comparisons
  (breakeven inflation rates, country GDP-share ratios, etc.). Drag in
  whole; edit inline by clicking the tile.
- **Maps tab**: build a map without leaving the page. Pick
  geography first, then indicator, then optional state(s), color
  scheme, and an area-of-interest bbox.
- **Generators tab**: mass-compose. Pick one series template
  ("nonfarm payroll employment", "median household income", "CO2 emissions per
  capita") plus a set of MSAs / states / countries, and the composer
  drops one tile per entity into your section. Useful for "unemployment
  across all 51 states" or "GDP per capita across every G20 country".

### Saving + sharing

- **Just share the URL** for any dashboard with under 50 entries
  or so, or make an account to enable custom URLs and unlimited
  length.
- **Accounts**: make an account using Google to save dashboards
  you've created and make them easily accessible, or to edit a copy
  of a dashboard someone else shared with you.
- **Set as default**: on the home page row actions for any saved
  dashboard. Pins it to a hero card above the list so it's a
  one-click open on every home-page visit.
- **Make a copy**: clones a dashboard to a fresh slug. Useful for
  forking a curated dashboard like this one and editing it without
  losing the original.

### Citation rigor

- Every chart has a source label + a link out to the upstream
  provider (FRED, World Bank, Census, BLS, etc.). The expanded chart
  dialog shows the data file path so anyone reading your saved
  dashboard can verify the numbers themselves.
- **Per-source pages** at `/source/<provider>/<series>/` carry a full
  citation block: provider name, series ID, license, last-updated
  date, frequency, and a copy-paste APA citation. Click the chart
  source label to land there.
- Data is refreshed weekly (market data) or monthly (demographics).
  Each refresh commits to git, so any historical vintage of any
  series can be resurrected from the repo's commit log if a
  researcher ever needs to reproduce a chart at the value it had on
  a specific date.

### Embed a chart in a piece

- Open the expanded view of any chart and copy the `<iframe>`
  snippet, it renders the chart on your blog, Substack, or research
  note, and auto-updates as the underlying series refreshes (no
  re-export needed).
- Embeds keep the same window pills + tooltip + source link the
  live chart has, so readers can interact with the data without
  leaving your page.

### Alerts when a series moves

- The [alerts page](/alerts/) sets email alerts on any source:
  greater-than, less-than, crosses-above, crosses-below, or
  absolute-change thresholds. Tape evaluates on every data refresh
  and emails on the cross.
- Smart-dispatch auto-pauses an alert that fires repeatedly without
  acknowledgement, so a chronically-tripping threshold doesn't
  bury your inbox.
