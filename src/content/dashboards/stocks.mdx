## What this dashboard is for

Individual-stock charts are usually presented as picks ("buy this, sell
that"). That's not the lens here. **These are sector-thesis vehicles.** A
view on the consumer goes through Walmart and McDonald's; a view on
healthcare costs goes through UnitedHealth; a view on AI capex goes
through TSMC and ASML.

## Reading patterns across sectors

- **Financials vs the yield curve.** When the 10Y–2Y spread inverts and
  stays inverted, banks (especially regional and money-center like BAC)
  underperform because their core net-interest-margin business gets
  squeezed. The 2022–24 inversion cycle is visible in JPM and BAC.

- **Healthcare bifurcation.** Pharma giants (LLY, PFE) trade on
  drug-pipeline catalysts; insurers (UNH) trade on Medicare-policy and
  utilization trends. They're in the same dashboard section but their
  drivers barely overlap.

- **Energy as macro proxy.** XOM and CVX are basically two ways to express
  the same view: oil up vs oil down. The 2022 spike and 2023 normalization
  are clean.

- **Consumer staples vs discretionary.** During inflation shocks, KO/PEP/
  WMT tend to outperform HD/MCD/DIS because consumers shift from
  discretionary to essential spending. This is a relative-trade story
  that's easier to see across the section than in any single chart.

## What this dashboard isn't

This isn't a portfolio. It's a tour of how individual large-cap stocks
serve as windows onto bigger themes. If you want a sector ETF view, the
composer has XLF, XLV, XLE, XLP, XLY, XLI, XLK and the rest of the GICS
sector ETFs ready to compose with. If you want full position-tracking,
that's not what Tape does — pick a brokerage tool for that.

What we offer that those tools don't: every chart shows the level *and*
the change, side-by-side, with curator notes explaining what to look at.
"Customize this dashboard" opens this list in the composer so you can
build your own variant.
